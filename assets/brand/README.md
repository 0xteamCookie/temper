## Idea

To temper steel is to strike it and then cool it under control, which makes it tougher. The mark is a T drawn as a forge hammer with a spark at the point of impact: changes land only through a controlled step, the per-hunk review.

## Rules

- **Colour.** Indigo `#4F39F6` is the only accent, on white `#FFFFFF`, paper `#F7F8FE` or ink `#1D2030`. Spark tint `#C9C2FF`. No gradients, glows or extra hues.
- **Wordmark.** Always lowercase `temper`, drawn from 7-unit round strokes; it is geometry, not a font, so do not retype it.
- **Clear space.** Keep at least half the mark's width empty around the logo.
- **Minimum size.** Mark 16 px; logo 96 px wide.
- Do not rotate, recolour, outline or add effects to the mark.
